Safety First

Designed for Safe Enforcement

Precision Targeting

V-Gun uses directional signal that targets specific vehicle IDs. No broadcast. No collateral effect. Only the intended vehicle receives the stop command.

  • Unique V-ID per vehicle
  • Directional beam technology
  • Confirmation before execution

Gradual Deceleration

The Vehicle Module doesn't slam brakes. It gradually reduces engine power over 10-15 seconds, allowing safe deceleration without losing steering or causing accidents.

  • Engine power reduction, not brake lock
  • Steering remains functional
  • Hazard lights auto-activate

Authority-Only Access

V-Gun units are authority-grade hardware with cryptographic authentication. Each command is logged with officer ID, timestamp, and GPS location for full accountability.

  • Biometric officer authentication
  • Command audit trail
  • Override protocols for emergencies

Non-Violent Enforcement

No physical force. No chase. No risk to bystanders. The vehicle stops itself under its own control, eliminating dangerous high-speed pursuits.

  • Zero physical contact
  • No high-speed chase required
  • Reduced risk for all parties
