Flash Pass (Legitimate Use)

Quick flashes under 500ms are legitimate driving communication - passing signals, warnings, or greetings. These are allowed without restriction.

Sustained Block (Hazardous Use)

Holding high beams blinds oncoming drivers. After 500ms, the Vehicle Module cuts the high beam circuit, forcing automatic dip.
