Our Mission

Making Roads Smart,
So Drivers Don't Have To Be.

India loses over 150,000 lives annually to road accidents. Many are caused by simple violations - honking in hospital zones, blinding high beams, ignoring stop signals.

We believe the solution isn't more cameras or more challans. It's moving enforcement from passive signage to active, hardware-level control. When the vehicle itself enforces the rule, compliance becomes inevitable.

V-orra isn't a traffic company. We're an infrastructure company. We're building the nervous system for India's roads.
